Inside a test cycle

Every cycle ends in a decision and a record

  1. IdentifyBarcode or QR code reads the serial number
  2. Load recipeParameters and limits set by part number
  3. FixturePart located and clamped in its own fixture
  4. TestMeasurements taken against the set sequence
  5. DecideAutomatic accept or reject against limits
  6. RecordResult stored against the serial number
AcceptPart moves on, with its result on file
RejectPart marked or set aside, so it stays out of good production
Quality dataOn-screen, printed or exported in the format your quality team needs
The value is in the record. When a question comes up from the field, the serial number leads straight back to the test result. The sequence varies by machine and follows what your quality system expects.

How a project runs

You approve the design, then you approve the trial

  1. RequirementsScope and assumptions agreed in writing, with a budgetary quote
  2. DesignConcept, 3D CAD and drawings, frozen with you before material is orderedYou approve
  3. BuildMachining, mechanical assembly, control panel and wiring
  4. ProgramPLC, HMI, recipes, alarms and data logging
  5. TestTrial runs, on your parts wherever possible, recorded against your requirementsYou approve
  6. DeliverInstallation, commissioning, training and handover documents

Nothing is built until you approve the design. Nothing ships until you accept the trial results.

You keep control at the two points that matter. Delivery schedule and milestones are stated in the quote. You are welcome to witness the trial before you approve shipment.
